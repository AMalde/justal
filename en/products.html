als products

I want to make products you’ll use every day — without pulling you out of the real world. In fact, I’d rather they free up more time for living: seeing friends, exploring outside, or just taking a breather.

I build these tools mostly by myself, with help from new tech that make it easier for one person to make something worth sharing. But even so, I believe in listening to you, the users, because you know what you need better than I could.

I also think it’s fair to get paid for making something people find useful, but not so much that the maker can afford to buy entire governments. A to-do app, no matter how magical, isn’t on the same level as food, water, or medicine. So you can pay what feels right, and once you own a product, it’s yours — no forced subscriptions.
